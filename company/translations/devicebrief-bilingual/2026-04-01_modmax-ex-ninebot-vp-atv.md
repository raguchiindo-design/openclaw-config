# 前九号产品副总裁创业，用“模块化”重构全地形车赛道，完成千万美元天使轮融资

近日，智能电动全地形车研发商魔迈（ModMax）宣布完成千万美元级种子轮及天使轮融资，投资方为高瓴创投、云时资本、云沐资本。资金将用于首款产品研发生产、团队搭建及海外品牌拓展。

## 创始团队背景
创始人林骥曾任九号公司（Segway-Ninebot）产品副总裁及赛格威创新事业部总经理，主导过九号卡丁系列、超级滑板车GT系列及E-bike系列等多款爆款产品，累计操盘营收数十亿元。团队核心成员多来自九号、大疆（DJI）等硬件科技独角兽企业。

## 市场切入点
全地形车（ATV/UTV）市场规模达百亿美元，长期被北极星（Polaris）、庞巴迪（BRP）、本田（Honda）和雅马哈（Yamaha）四大巨头垄断，市场集中度高达97%。

在欧美市场，全地形车既是农场和牧场生产力工具，也是越野爱好者的运动玩具。目前市场正面临需求侧变化：零排放、低噪音、智能化的电动产品更受年轻一代户外爱好者青睐。然而，行业龙头北极星直到2023年才推出首款电动UTV，且性能与智能化水平未达预期。

## 产品逻辑：模块化与软件定义
魔迈（ModMax）试图通过“模块化”硬件架构和自研智能电控算法，将全地形车从“机械猛兽”重塑为“智能运动平台”。

1. **模块化设计**：利用电动系统灵活性打造高冗余、可扩展硬件。用户可根据场景（如森林狩猎、沙漠穿越、农场运货）自由改装功能模块。
2. **场景解耦**：电动化让车辆能同时具备“静音工具”和“性能玩具”属性。
3. **自研电控算法**：针对极端越野环境下电机瞬时扭矩过大易失控的问题，通过精准算法实现线性牵引。

## Device Brief English Article

# Ex-Ninebot VP Reimagines All-Terrain Vehicles: ModMax Secures $10M to Disrupt a Century-Old Industry

**ModMax (魔迈)**, a rising player in the intelligent electric All-Terrain Vehicle (ATV) sector, has recently announced the completion of multi-million dollar Seed and Angel funding rounds. The investment, led by **Hillhouse Capital (高瓴创投)**, **Cloud Time Capital (云时资本)**, and **Cloud Mu Capital (云沐资本)**, will fuel the R&D of its debut product and its expansion into global markets.

### The Team Behind the "New Species"
Founded by **Lin Ji (林骥)**, the former VP of Product at **Ninebot (Segway-Ninebot)** and GM of Segway’s Innovation Division, ModMax brings a powerhouse of hardware expertise. Lin previously spearheaded the development of viral hits like the Ninebot GoKart, the GT scooter series, and several E-bike lines, managing billions in revenue. The core team is largely comprised of veterans from hardware unicorns like **Ninebot** and **DJI**, bringing a "move fast and break things" mentality to a traditionally conservative industry.

### Cracking a $10 Billion Monopoly
The global ATV/UTV (Utility Task Vehicle) market is a $10 billion arena that has been dominated for decades by four giants: **Polaris**, **BRP (Bombardier)**, **Honda**, and **Yamaha**. These players control a staggering 97% of the market. 

In North America alone, millions of farms and ranches rely on these vehicles as essential productivity tools, while off-road enthusiasts treat them as heavy-duty toys for recreation. However, a massive shift is occurring. Younger outdoor enthusiasts are demanding zero-emission, low-noise, and intelligent vehicles—needs that the incumbents have been slow to address. Polaris, for instance, only launched its first electric UTV in 2023, leaving a significant window for more agile, tech-driven startups.

### Hardware Modularization & "Software-Defined" Off-Roading
Unlike traditional "gas-to-electric" conversions, ModMax is rebuilding the ATV from the ground up using a **modular hardware architecture** and proprietary smart control algorithms.

1. **The "Lego" of ATVs**: ModMax utilizes the flexibility of electric drivetrains to create a highly redundant, expandable platform. Users can swap modules to adapt the vehicle for different scenarios—whether it's forest hunting, desert racing, or farm transport.
2. **Decoupling Use Cases**: In the internal combustion era, a vehicle was usually locked into a single persona. ModMax’s electric architecture allows a single vehicle to serve as both a silent workhorse for the morning and a high-performance sport machine for the afternoon.
3. **Smart Torque Management**: One of the biggest hurdles in electric off-roading is managing the instant torque of electric motors on slippery or rocky terrain. ModMax has developed custom algorithms to ensure linear traction, preventing wheel spin and allowing for precise control in extreme environments.

### The China Advantage in "Overtaking on the Curve"
While Chinese companies were historically followers in the internal combustion engine era, the shift to electric plays directly into China’s strengths. With over 70% of the world’s lithium battery production and a world-leading supply chain for motors and intelligent driving sensors, Chinese startups like ModMax are well-positioned to redefine the global ATV landscape.
