# 2026-03-11 | Funding Brief | Embodied AI, Exoskeletons, Smart Sofas & Sleep Tech Scale Up

## 标题（中文）
融资简报｜具身智能、外骨骼、智能沙发与睡眠科技加速落地

## Title (English)
Funding Brief | Embodied AI, Exoskeletons, Smart Sofas & Sleep Tech Scale Up

## 副标题（中文）
从10亿元级融资到百亿估值，量产与商业化信号正在推动 Physical AI 从概念走向落地。

## Subtitle (English)
RMB 1B rounds, RMB 10B+ valuations, and product-ready deployment signals show Physical AI moving from concept to commercialization.

---

中文原文：
2026年第一波 AI 硬件融资，不再只是“演示效果”的竞争，而是围绕部署能力、分发效率与可防御产品系统的竞争，覆盖机器人、外骨骼、智能家居与睡眠科技。

English translation:
The first wave of 2026 AI hardware funding is no longer about demos alone — it is about deployment, distribution, and defensible product systems across robotics, exoskeletons, smart home, and sleep tech.

中文原文：
1）济佳时界（Jijia Shijie）完成约10亿元人民币Pre-B轮融资，用于推进具身基础模型与世界模型演进，并继续落地“具身基座模型、世界模型、原生具身、泛化场景”四位一体策略。

English translation:
1) Jijia Shijie has completed an approximately RMB 1 billion Pre-B round to advance embodied foundation models and world models under its four-part strategy: embodied base model, world model, native embodiment, and generalized scenarios.

中文原文：
2）星动纪元（Xingdong Jiyuan）再获约10亿元战略轮融资，估值突破100亿元人民币，产业投资人数量达16家，被视为中国具身智能赛道中产业协同能力最强的团队之一。

English translation:
2) Xingdong Jiyuan raised another RMB 1 billion strategic round and crossed RMB 10 billion in valuation, with 16 industrial investors on its cap table, making it one of the strongest industry-backed embodied-intelligence startups in China.

中文原文：
3）程天科技（Chengtian Keji）宣布完成1亿元以上B+轮融资，重点推动消费级外骨骼场景扩张、具身智能外骨骼研发，以及脑机相关产品注册和渠道建设。

English translation:
3) Chengtian Keji announced an RMB 100M+ B+ round to expand consumer exoskeleton markets, deepen embodied-intelligence exoskeleton R&D, and accelerate BCI-related product registration and channels.

中文原文：
4）舒曼德（Smarter）完成近1亿元人民币融资，试图把AI自适应沙发打造成连接人体工学硬件、非接触健康感知与消费级智能家居落地的中间层产品。

English translation:
4) Smarter Shumande has reportedly completed financing of nearly RMB 100 million and is positioning AI adaptive sofas as a bridge between ergonomic hardware, contactless health sensing, and consumer-ready smart home deployment.

中文原文：
5）Eight Sleep 在 Tether Investments 领投下完成5000万美元战略融资，估值达到15亿美元，进一步巩固其在 AI 睡眠硬件与传感系统中的产品定义权。

English translation:
5) Eight Sleep raised $50 million in a strategic round led by Tether Investments, reaching a $1.5 billion valuation and further reinforcing its category leadership in AI-enabled sleep hardware.

中文原文：
这轮融资背后的核心信号是：资本正在奖励能把“模型能力”闭环到“产品经济性”的团队。下一阶段 AI 竞争，将主要发生在真实物理世界的产品与系统中。

English translation:
The core signal is clear: capital is now rewarding teams that can close the loop from model capability to product economics. The next phase of AI competition will be fought in the physical world.
