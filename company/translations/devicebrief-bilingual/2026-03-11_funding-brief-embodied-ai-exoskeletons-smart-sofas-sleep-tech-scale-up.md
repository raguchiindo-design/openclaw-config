# 2026-03-11_funding-brief-embodied-ai-exoskeletons-smart-sofas-sleep-tech-scale-up

## 中文原文（全文）
2026年第一波 AI 硬件融资，不再只是“演示效果”的竞争，而是围绕部署能力、分发效率与可防御产品系统的竞争，覆盖机器人、外骨骼、智能家居与睡眠科技。

1）济佳时界（Jijia Shijie）完成约10亿元人民币Pre-B轮融资，用于推进具身基础模型与世界模型演进，并继续落地“具身基座模型、世界模型、原生具身、泛化场景”四位一体策略。

2）星动纪元（Xingdong Jiyuan）再获约10亿元战略轮融资，估值突破100亿元人民币，产业投资人数量达16家，被视为中国具身智能赛道中产业协同能力最强的团队之一。

3）程天科技（Chengtian Keji）宣布完成1亿元以上B+轮融资，重点推动消费级外骨骼场景扩张、具身智能外骨骼研发，以及脑机相关产品注册和渠道建设。

4）舒曼德（Smarter）完成近1亿元人民币融资，试图把AI自适应沙发打造成连接人体工学硬件、非接触健康感知与消费级智能家居落地的中间层产品。

5）Eight Sleep 在 Tether Investments 领投下完成5000万美元战略融资，估值达到15亿美元，进一步巩固其在 AI 睡眠硬件与传感系统中的产品定义权。

这轮融资背后的核心信号是：资本正在奖励能把“模型能力”闭环到“产品经济性”的团队。下一阶段 AI 竞争，将主要发生在真实物理世界的产品与系统中。

## English Translation (Full)
The first wave of 2026 AI hardware funding is no longer about demo quality alone. Competition is now centered on deployment capability, distribution efficiency, and defensible product systems across robotics, exoskeletons, smart home, and sleep tech.

1) Jijia Shijie completed an approximately RMB 1 billion Pre-B round to advance embodied foundation models and world models, while continuing its four-part strategy: embodied base model, world model, native embodiment, and generalized scenarios.

2) Xingdong Jiyuan raised another RMB 1 billion strategic round and surpassed a RMB 10 billion valuation. With 16 industrial investors, it is widely viewed as one of the strongest industry-collaboration teams in China’s embodied-intelligence track.

3) Chengtian Keji announced an RMB 100M+ B+ round, focused on expanding consumer exoskeleton scenarios, deepening embodied-intelligence exoskeleton R&D, and accelerating registration and channel development for BCI-related products.

4) Shumande (Smarter) reportedly completed financing of nearly RMB 100 million, positioning its AI adaptive sofa as a bridge product connecting ergonomic hardware, contactless health sensing, and consumer-grade smart home deployment.

5) Eight Sleep completed a $50 million strategic round led by Tether Investments, reaching a $1.5 billion valuation and further strengthening its category leadership in AI sleep hardware and sensing systems.

The core signal behind this funding cycle is clear: capital is rewarding teams that can close the loop from model capability to product economics. The next stage of AI competition will be fought in real-world physical products and systems.
