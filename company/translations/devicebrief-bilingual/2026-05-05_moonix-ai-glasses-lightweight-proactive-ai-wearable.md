## 2) 清洗后的纯中文正文

# 莫奈 MOONIX AI 眼镜首发信息整理

Dreame Next 大会首日，一副重量仅 14.9g 的 AI 眼镜吸引了现场关注。这款 MOONIX（莫奈）AI 眼镜出自追觅投资的同名品牌 MOONIX。它的核心卖点不是单一的拍摄或录音，而是在轻量化眼镜形态中，把录音、录像、空间音频和主动式 AI Agent 组合成一个随身个人数据入口。

## 标准版：14.9g，强调“回归眼镜本身”

莫奈 AI 眼镜标准版整机重量仅 14.9 克，镜腿最薄处约 4mm。相比很多 AI 眼镜常见的厚重镜框，它在外观上更接近日常眼镜，强调轻薄、纤细和佩戴接受度。

标准版配备 6 麦克风阵列和一对空间音频扬声器，支持全向高清拾音和全天候待机。电池采用双电池设计，两条镜腿各内置 50mAh 电池，以提升重量平衡和续航稳定性。

## Pro 版：17.9g，加入 1080p 摄像头

带摄像头的 Pro 版本重量为 17.9g，镜腿厚度约 5mm。摄像头位于镜腿处，规格为 1080p 全高清摄像头，官方强调其低功耗设计，并支持 Always-On 全天候录制与高频拍摄。

Pro 版同样配备六麦克风阵列，支持录音、录像、空间音频与全天候续航。

## 模块化设计与镜框选择

两个版本都采用分体式设计，支持前镜框替换。产品上市时预计将提供上百款前镜框选择，并计划推出乔布斯特别款。

## 主动式 AI Agent：基于个人数据的随身 AI 入口

莫奈 AI 眼镜的核心能力是 Proactive AI Agent（主动式 AI Agent）。其工作方式是“无感记录 + AI 生成”：眼镜以低打扰方式记录用户看到的内容、对话场景和到过的地点，再基于这些个人上下文数据主动生成提醒、建议或备忘。

这意味着产品定位并不只是“拍摄眼镜”或“录音眼镜”，而是试图成为一个基于个人数据持续学习的 AI 入口。其关键挑战也会集中在佩戴舒适度、隐私边界、续航、数据管理和用户对全天候记录的接受度。

## 发售信息

标准版预计 6 月上市，定价约在 2500 元以上；Pro 版预计 8 月上市。


## 3) Device Brief 英文发表稿

# MOONIX AI Glasses Push the Wearable AI Race Toward Lighter, More Personal Devices

**Subtitle:** Backed by Dreame, MOONIX is positioning its first AI glasses around lightweight industrial design, always-available capture, and a proactive AI agent built on personal context.

Dreame’s latest product event included a notable signal for the AI hardware market: MOONIX, a brand backed by Dreame, introduced a pair of AI glasses that are trying to move the category away from bulky “tech-on-your-face” hardware and closer to something people might actually wear every day.

The headline specification is weight. The standard MOONIX AI Glasses weigh just 14.9 grams, with the thinnest part of the temple measuring about 4mm. That matters because many AI glasses still look and feel like prototypes: thick frames, heavy temples, and visible compromises around cameras, batteries, speakers, and microphones. MOONIX is taking the opposite route, emphasizing a slim frame and a more conventional eyewear profile.

The standard version is built around audio-first AI use cases. It includes a six-microphone array for omnidirectional high-definition voice pickup, plus a pair of spatial audio speakers. The glasses are designed for always-available standby, so the device can respond whenever the user needs it. Power is split across two batteries, with one 50mAh battery in each temple, a design choice that should help with both balance and endurance.

MOONIX is also preparing a Pro version with a camera. Even with a 1080p full-HD camera built into the temple, the Pro model is listed at only 17.9 grams, with a roughly 5mm temple thickness. The company says the camera module uses a low-power design and supports always-on recording and high-frequency capture. Combined with the same six-microphone setup, the Pro version is positioned as a richer capture device for both audio and video contexts.

Both versions use a split-frame architecture with replaceable front frames. At launch, MOONIX expects to offer more than 100 front-frame options, including a Steve Jobs-inspired special edition. This modularity is not just a style feature: for AI glasses, frame choice can directly affect adoption, because users are unlikely to wear a device all day if it does not match their face, prescription needs, or personal style.

The more important product claim is the AI layer. MOONIX says both versions will include a Proactive AI Agent. Instead of waiting for every command, the glasses are designed to quietly collect personal context — what the user sees, what they discuss, and where they go — and then generate reminders, suggestions, or notes based on that context.

That places MOONIX in a broader shift in wearable AI: the device is no longer just a recorder, camera, or audio assistant. It is being framed as a personal data interface. The value proposition is that the glasses become more useful as they understand the user’s routines, conversations, and environment over time.

This approach also raises the hard questions every always-on AI wearable will face. A 14.9g or 17.9g form factor is impressive, but adoption will depend on more than weight. Users will need clear privacy controls, predictable battery life, reliable data management, and social comfort around recording in public and private settings. The technology may be ready to shrink; the trust model still has to mature.

MOONIX currently plans to launch the standard version in June, with pricing expected above RMB 2,500. The Pro version is expected to arrive in August.

For Device Brief, the key takeaway is that AI glasses are entering a new phase. The most competitive products will not simply add bigger cameras or louder AI claims. They will need to balance wearable design, personal context, battery constraints, and privacy expectations — all while making the device feel normal enough to wear outside a demo booth.
