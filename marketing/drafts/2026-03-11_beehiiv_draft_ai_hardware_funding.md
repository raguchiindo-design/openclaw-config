# Funding Brief | Embodied AI, Exoskeletons, Smart Sofas & Sleep Tech Scale Up

**Subtitle:** RMB 1B rounds, RMB 10B+ valuations, and product-ready deployment signals show Physical AI moving from concept to commercialization.

The first wave of 2026 AI hardware funding is no longer about demos alone — it is about deployment, distribution, and defensible product systems across robotics, exoskeletons, smart home, and sleep tech.

**1) Jijia Shijie: ~$138M Pre-B to scale embodied foundation models**

Jijia Shijie, founded by former Horizon executives, has completed an approximately RMB 1 billion (about $138 million) Pre-B round. The company says proceeds will support the evolution of embodied foundation models and world models under its four-part strategy: embodied base model, world model, native embodiment, and generalized scenarios.

*Image note: Jijia Shijie — embodied intelligence/robotics company visual (source: company/media release image).*

**2) Xingdong Jiyuan: Another ~$138M raised, valuation above ~$1.38B**

Xingdong Jiyuan has closed an RMB 1 billion (about $138 million) strategic round and crossed the RMB 10 billion (about $1.38 billion) valuation mark. With 16 industrial investors now on cap table, it is widely seen as one of the strongest industry-backed embodied-intelligence startups in China.

*Image note: Xingdong Jiyuan / RobotEra — humanoid robotics product visual (source: company official image).*

**3) Chengtian Keji: RMB 100M+ (about $13.8M+) B+ to push exoskeletons from clinics to homes**

Chengtian Keji announced an RMB 100M+ (about $13.8M+) B+ round to expand consumer exoskeleton markets, deepen embodied-intelligence exoskeleton R&D, and accelerate BCI-related product registration and channels.

**4) Shumande (Smarter): Financing near RMB 100M (~$13.8M)**

Smarter Shumande has reportedly completed financing of nearly RMB 100 million (about $13.8 million). The company is positioning AI adaptive sofas as a bridge between ergonomic hardware, contactless health sensing, and consumer-ready smart home deployment.

**5) Eight Sleep: Tether-backed round values company at $1.5B**

Eight Sleep has raised $50 million in a strategic round led by Tether Investments, taking valuation to $1.5 billion. Its Pod line continues to define the category of AI-enabled, sensor-driven sleep systems.

*Image note: Eight Sleep Pod — AI-enabled sleep hardware visual (source: Eight Sleep press image).*

**Why this matters**

Capital is now rewarding teams that can close the loop from model capability to product economics: better task success, better user outcomes, and faster commercialization. The next phase of AI competition will be fought in the physical world.
